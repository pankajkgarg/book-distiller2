## Argument arc

The fundamentals establish why small habits compound, why identity matters more than isolated outcomes, and how the cue-craving-response-reward loop works. The body turns that model into four laws—make it obvious, attractive, easy, and satisfying—then closes with advanced questions of talent, motivation, and the downside of automaticity.
